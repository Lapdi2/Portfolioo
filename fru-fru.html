FRU FRU

PORTABLE BLENDER

YEAR: April – June 2024
DESIGNERS: Samuele D’Auria, Edoardo Gelmetti, Emma Listorto, Giorgia Melloni
MY ROLE & SKILLS: Sketching, concept development and prototyping
COURSE: Design Fundamentals Studio, First Year, Politecnico di Milano
LECTURERS: C. Bruno, M. De Liguori, E. Messineo, V. Rognoli

BRIEF: A compact and portable blender designed to mix fruit, liquids, and powdered supplements. Developed for fitness and sports environments, it allows users to prepare a fresh drink on demand before or after their workout.

FRU FRU is a 2-in-1 portable blender designed for everyday use on the go, combining the functions of a water bottle and a blender into one compact product. The concept is based on a modular system where the motorised base, which integrates the blade and motor, can be separated from the container, allowing it to function as a regular bottle when blending is not needed.
