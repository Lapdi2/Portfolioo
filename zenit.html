ZENIT

YOGA SHELF

YEAR: September 2024 – January 2025
DESIGNERS: Corsaro Paolo Akira, Ferri Linda, Listorto Emma, Melloni Giorgia, Romagnoli Desiree, Santos Martina
MY ROLE & SKILLS: Concept development, 3D modelling, rendering, technical drawings, product communication
COURSE: Meta-Design Studio, Second Year, Politecnico di Milano
LECTURERS: Beatrice Villari, Marco Ajovalasit

BRIEF:
Design a piece of furniture that helps young people create an environment suitable for practicing yoga and meditation, and stores the accessories needed for these activities.

Zenit is a piece of furniture designed to provide an ideal setting for yoga and meditation, tailored for those with limited time and space. Combining aesthetics and functionality, it stores the essentials for these disciplines while creating the perfect atmosphere for their practice.
