LEGO IDEAS SUBMISSION

YEAR: August 2025
DESIGNERS: Emma Listorto
MY ROLE & SKILLS: Concept development, sketching, 3D modelling, rendering and product communication

BRIEF:
LEGO® Ideas is a platform that gives fans the opportunity to become set designers. Users can submit their own models and, if they receive enough support from the community, their ideas can become official LEGO® products and enter production.

The set is called Cat Café & Art Supplies Shop and was uploaded to the LEGO® Ideas website to collect votes. It’s a modular set consisting of two adjacent buildings, each with two floors. The roofs and rooms can be separated for play with minifigures, while its modularity allows it to connect to other sets in the series.
